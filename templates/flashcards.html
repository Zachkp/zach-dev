How it works

Two ways to build a deck — pick whichever's faster.

Upload a CSV

  1. 1

    Make a spreadsheet with two columns: the term in the first, its definition in the second.

  2. 2

    Export or save it as a .csv file. A header row like "term,definition" is fine but optional.

  3. 3

    Drop the file on Upload CSV above and hit Upload deck to start studying.

Create a deck here

  1. 1

    Switch to Create a deck and type a term and definition into each row.

  2. 2

    Press Enter to jump to the next field, or Shift+Enter for a line break. Add as many cards as you like.

  3. 3

    Hit Save & study to download a copy and start flipping. Already have a CSV? Use Import CSV to keep editing it.

While studying, tap a card to flip it, and use the arrows to move through the deck or shuffle the order.
